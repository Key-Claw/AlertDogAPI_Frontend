Comprehensive Wellness Exams

Regular check-ups are the foundation of your pet's health. Our thorough wellness exams help detect issues early and keep your furry friend happy and healthy for years to come.

Book Exam Contact Us

What to Expect

During a wellness exam, our veterinarians perform a nose-to-tail assessment of your pet. We check their eyes, ears, teeth, heart, lungs, skin, and coat. We also discuss their diet, exercise routine, and behavior to ensure a holistic approach to their well-being.

  • Comprehensive physical examination
  • Vaccination review and updates
  • Parasite prevention consultation
  • Nutritional counseling

Why It Matters

Pets age much faster than humans, so annual (or semi-annual for seniors) exams are crucial. Early detection of conditions like dental disease, diabetes, or arthritis can significantly improve treatment outcomes and quality of life.
