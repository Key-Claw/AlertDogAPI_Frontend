About Dr. Jenkins

Dr. Sarah Jenkins has been practicing veterinary medicine for over 12 years. Her passion for animals began at a young age, volunteering at local shelters. She believes in a compassionate, fear-free approach to veterinary care, ensuring every pet feels safe and loved during their visit.

Specialties

  • Internal Medicine
  • Soft Tissue Surgery
  • Geriatric Care

Education

  • DVM, Doctor of Veterinary Medicine University of California, Davis
  • BS, Animal Science Cornell University

Philosophy

"I treat every patient as if they were my own pet. My goal is to partner with pet parents to provide the longest, happiest, and healthiest lives possible for their furry family members."
